## Conversion: naive vs clustered

Baseline conversion 0.5259; MDE 1.0% relative (0.526pp); alpha 0.05 two-sided; power 0.8.
Pre-period design effect 1.78 at m_eff 9.3 sessions, implying intra-customer correlation 0.0933.

| | Naive binomial | Clustered (delta method) |
|---|---|---|
| Required sessions per arm | 141,504 | 208,528 |
| Days to reach 80% power | 13 | 18 |
| MDE at 56 days | 0.43% | 0.67% |

The naive calculation understates the required sample by a factor of 1.47 (the design effect at that duration) and the duration by 5 days. At the full 56 days the design effect is 2.42, so naive standard errors would be 1.56x too narrow.

Steady-state window (weeks 3-8, used for the business case): 1,185,085 sessions, design effect 2.09, MDE 0.49% naive vs 0.71% clustered.
